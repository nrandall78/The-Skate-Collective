Our Founder

BUILT FROM
THE CULTURE.

The Skate Collective was founded by Nicole Randall — skater, veteran, community builder, and entrepreneur based in Dallas, TX.

Nicole served 20 years in the United States Air Force — bringing the same discipline, dedication, and service mindset to everything she builds. She's been on skates since she was a little girl, and that love never left her.

She created The Skate Collective because she saw a gap — no single place existed where skaters worldwide could find events, connect, and celebrate the culture together.

This is her answer to that gap. Built by a skater, for skaters — everywhere.

The vision
"I wanted one place where any skater — from Atlanta to Paris to São Paulo — could find their people, find their next event, and feel like they belong. That's The Skate Collective."
— Nicole Randall, Founder
